About TYPEFIRE

What is TYPEFIRE?

TYPEFIRE is a minimalist typing speed test for anyone who wants to measure and improve their words-per-minute (WPM) and accuracy. It's fast, distraction-free, and works on any device — no account required.

How it works

Choose a duration — 15, 30, or 60 seconds — then start typing. Passages are drawn from a curated library of science, mathematics, and philosophy texts, so every session feels fresh and intellectually engaging.

Passages

All typing passages are sourced from English Wikipedia articles on topics in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, philosophy, logic, and related fields. Passages are filtered to ensure they are well-formed English prose — no formulas, no dialogue, no lists.

Leaderboard

After each round, enter a nickname to submit your score to the global leaderboard. Compete with players around the world and see where you stand.

Sharing

Tap the SHARE button on the results screen to generate a score card image you can share on social media or send to friends.

Tips to improve

  • Focus on accuracy first — speed follows naturally
  • Keep your eyes on the word display, not your fingers
  • Try the 15s mode for a quick warm-up anytime
  • Practice daily, even a single 30-second session builds the habit
  • Try the 60s mode to push your stamina and consistency
